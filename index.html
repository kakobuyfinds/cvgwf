From discovery to a considered purchase

How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet organizes possible purchases. It does not confirm stock, quality or whether a particular agent can currently purchase an item.

1

Save the exact item

Record the original listing, seller, size, color and quoted currency. Keep the specific variant beside its link.

2

Check the fit

Compare the seller’s measurements with a garment or pair of shoes you already use. Ask about missing dimensions.

3

Confirm purchasing

Ask your chosen agent about current item acceptance, fees, return conditions and inspection options before paying.

4

Review the parcel

Check inspection photos where provided, then confirm packaging, billable weight and the final shipping quote.

Better comparison notes

What to record before ordering

Two similar photographs can hide different measurements, quantities or materials. Build a shortlist around comparable details and keep unanswered questions visible.

Suggested fields for your personal shopping sheet
CategoryUseful checks
Shoes & slidesSize system, foot-length guidance, width and selected color.
Hoodies & T-shirtsChest width, length, shoulder width and stated fabric composition.
JacketsChest and sleeve measurements, lining, closure and layering room.
Bags & accessoriesDimensions, material statements, included parts and compatibility.

Inspection and listing checklist

  • Match the product, color and size to the order record.
  • Review available photos for visible damage, missing parts and obvious construction issues.
  • Request measurements or additional angles when the supplied photos leave a specific question unanswered.
  • Confirm return deadlines before authorizing shipment.
  • Save the seller link, agent order reference and dated answers together.

Listing photographs are not warehouse QC photos. Photographs alone do not establish authenticity, composition or long-term durability. This page has not inspected the listed products.

A few useful answers

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list used to organize item links, categories, prices and comparison notes. This page provides a small CAFANS catalog preview and a CSV export that you can expand with your own research.

Is this the official ACBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery page. The ACBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons lead to CAFANS. This page does not accept payments or manage orders, parcels or refunds.

Where do the products and prices come from?

The six previews use product names, images, item references and estimated USD prices from the CAFANS homepage. They form a static snapshot. Open each original product link to review current information; stock and final prices are not confirmed here.

Can every listed item be purchased through ACBuy?

Compatibility is not verified. A catalog entry does not establish that an agent currently accepts its marketplace, seller or product category. Confirm current purchasing availability directly with the agent before depositing money or submitting an order.

Are shipping and service charges included?

No confirmed shipping or service charges are included in these preview prices. Obtain a quote for your selected variant and destination, then record additional charges separately in your budget.

Does the preview include verified QC photos?

No. The images are CAFANS listing photographs. If your chosen agent provides warehouse inspection photos, review those for the specific item received and ask about unclear measurements or visible issues.

Can I download this as a spreadsheet?

Yes. The CSV button exports the currently visible products with their categories, estimated USD prices and original CAFANS links. Open it in a spreadsheet application and add your own sizing, quote and inspection notes.
